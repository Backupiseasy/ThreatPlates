# @project-version@ (@build-time@)

* Fixed a bug where a nameplate kept its target size, transparency, or color after changing or clearing the target, if the nameplate had been hidden and shown again while the unit was targeted [Comment #8666].
* Fixed a bug where a nameplate could keep its target size if the target was gained and lost again very quickly.
* Fixed a bug where nameplates kept their non-target size and transparency after the target or soft target was lost.
* Fixed a bug where auras on nameplates prevented mouseover of nameplates behind them, even with aura tooltips disabled (Classic only) [Comment #8131].
