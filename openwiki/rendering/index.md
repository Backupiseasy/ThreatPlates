# Files

- [Elements](elements.md) - The always-present building blocks of a Threat Plates nameplate - how ElementHandler registers and drives them, the lifecycle hooks, and what each element (healthbar with absorbs, name, status text, castbar, level, classification, threat glow, raid marker, mouseover highlight) does.
- [Modules: threat, color, transparency, scaling and helpers](modules.md) - The shared, dot-called Modules of Threat Plates - threat level detection, healthbar and name colors, transparency, scaling, fonts, icons, animations and localization helpers - with their inputs, events and calling order.
- [Styles and custom styles](styles.md) - How Threat Plates builds its themes, picks a style for every unit (visibility, headline view, custom style triggers, threat styles), applies a style to a nameplate in a fixed order, and runs scripted custom styles through the Script widget.
